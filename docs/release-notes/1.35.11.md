---REVIEW---
This update revises 44 classical Chinese prose texts so their wording, punctuation and paragraphing match the current national middle- and high-school Chinese textbooks, adds the author's preface to one text, and adds a variant-reading note to another. Translations, annotations and commentary for these texts were updated accordingly. No new permissions, no new third-party SDKs, no changes to accounts or data collection. The app works fully offline; account creation is optional. Test account is unchanged from the previous submission.
