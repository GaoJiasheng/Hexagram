观象 · 书桌 (Guanxiang Desk) is a native (SwiftUI / TextKit) study app for classical Chinese texts. It is a different product from our other app 观象 (pub.gavin.hexa), which is a web-wrapper library for browsing and searching 100+ texts. Desk holds only the few books the user picks and is built around paged reading, per-word annotations, highlights and notes, study-time statistics and weekly goals, and importing self-made books (.deskbook) - none of which exist in 观象. The two apps share the same optional account so that progress carries over.

Sign-in is optional: all reading features work without an account. The demo account above is the same one used for 观象. Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in are also offered.

How to test:
- Add books: Home > 添书 lists all 79 texts; tap one to download it.
- Read: tap a paragraph to show its translation; tap a dotted-underlined word to see its annotation; long-press text to highlight.
- Import a self-made book: the .deskbook format is open (https://hexa.gavin.pub/desk/standard/). Copy this link: https://hexa.gavin.pub/desk/standard/samples/sample-book.deskbook then Home > 添书 > import button (top left) > 粘贴书包链接. Imported books stay on the device and are never uploaded.
- Delete account: Home > account button (top right) > 注销账号.

Content notes: the Zhouyi (I Ching) and the nine traditional "命理" texts are presented only as original text with translation and annotation, for literary study; the app has no divination, fortune-telling or chart-casting features. The classical medical texts are for philological and historical reading only; the app gives no medical advice.

A "书房" (private shelf) section is visible only to the developer's own admin account and will not appear for the review account. No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
