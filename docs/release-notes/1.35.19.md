iPad 版改用 App 式布局，并改版全部典籍的目录页：
· iPad 首页改成一屏看全的「书房」：接着读、今日一句与十六组书架
· iPad 固定导航：竖屏在底部、横屏在左侧，书房 · 接着读 · 搜索 · 索引 · 我的
· 阅读时选章改为侧边滑出的目录面板；进入下一层后左上角有返回键
· 八十部典籍的目录页各配一幅单色水墨题图，随明暗主题自动变色
· 目录按书自己的结构分段；「透镜」点一个关键词，含该词的章亮起
· 接着读记到段；按钮与可点区域放大，更好点按
---REVIEW---
This update gives the iPad a tablet-specific layout: a fixed tab bar (bottom in portrait, side rail in landscape) with five sections, a one-screen home "study" view, a slide-out chapter list in the reader, and larger touch targets. It also redesigns the table-of-contents page of all 80 classical texts with monochrome ink illustrations, structure-aware chapter groups and keyword highlighting. All content is bundled and works offline; no new permissions, no new SDKs, no new data collection. Account creation remains optional. Test account unchanged.
