---REVIEW---
This update improves in-app search: results now show a short preview of the matching original text, and opening a result scrolls to the matched passage and briefly highlights it. It fixes a search bug where a result could open the wrong chapter after an update, expands the table of contents directly on the home pages of small collections, replaces on/off text buttons with standard toggle switches, and tidies card layouts and the mobile footer. No new permissions, no new third-party SDKs, no changes to accounts or data collection. The app works fully offline; account creation is optional. Test account is unchanged from the previous submission.
