新增「详注」:比原来的字词注释更密、更细的一层
· 点带下划线的词,先看一句话释义,下面再有一段展开:为什么这么讲、与今义的差别、各家怎么说
· 首批十部:道德经、庄子、论语、周易(六十四卦与系辞、说卦、序卦、杂卦)、孙子兵法、鬼谷子、周易参同契、阴符经、清静经、太上感应篇,其余典籍陆续补上
· 设置里新增两个开关:「字词注释」总开关,「详注」可单独关掉、退回原来较疏的注释
---REVIEW---
This update adds a denser annotation layer ("详注") to the bundled classical texts: tapping an underlined word now shows a one-line gloss followed by a short explanatory paragraph. Ten books have it in this version; more will follow. Two new switches in Settings turn word annotations on/off and choose between the detailed and the original sparse layer. No new permissions, no new data collection, no new third-party SDKs. All content is bundled and works offline. Account creation remains optional. Test account unchanged.
