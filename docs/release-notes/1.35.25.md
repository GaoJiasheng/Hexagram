---REVIEW---
This is a content-correction update. Over 1,500 bundled reading essays were re-checked paragraph by paragraph against the original classical texts, and about 5,800 factual details were corrected (misattributed quotations, miscounted passages, interpretations presented as settled fact). In the traditional-medicine section, a few sentences that read like health advice were removed; the app remains a study tool and gives no medical advice. No feature changes, no new permissions, no new data collection, no new third-party SDKs. All content is bundled and works offline. Account creation remains optional. Test account unchanged.
